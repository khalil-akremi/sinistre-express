# VERIDIA INSURANCE — DriveSafe Motor Policy
## General Conditions (fictional document, for demonstration only)

Veridia Insurance is a fictional company. This contract is written for a
software demonstration and has no legal value.

The contract is made of two parts:
- these **General Conditions**, which apply to every policyholder;
- the **Policy Schedule**, which lists the facts about one policyholder
  (coverages bought, vehicle, market value, driving licence date).
  If the two conflict, the Policy Schedule prevails.

All amounts are in euros. All deadlines are in calendar days.

---

### Article 1 — Definitions

- **Policyholder**: the person named in the Policy Schedule.
- **Authorised driver**: the policyholder, or anyone driving with their
  permission and holding a valid driving licence.
- **Third party**: any person other than the authorised driver.
- **Deductible**: the part of each claim that remains payable by the
  policyholder.
- **Market value**: the value of the insured vehicle, as stated in the
  Policy Schedule.
- **Repair cost**: the amount shown on the repair quote or invoice.

---

### Article 2 — Coverages

A coverage applies only if it is listed in the policyholder's Policy Schedule.
A claim under a coverage that is not in the Policy Schedule is not paid under
this contract, and is referred to a claims handler (see Article 7).

| Code | Coverage | What is covered | Standard deductible |
|------|----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| C1 | Third-party liability | Damage the authorised driver causes to a third party | €0 |
| C2 | Collision damage | Damage to the insured vehicle after a collision | €300 |
| C3 | Glass breakage | Windscreen, side windows and rear window | €50 |
| C4 | Theft | Theft of the whole insured vehicle | 10% of the claim, minimum €500 |

For theft (C4), the amount of the claim is the market value of the vehicle.

---

### Article 3 — Exclusions

No coverage applies when:

1. the driver had alcohol above the legal limit, or was under the influence
   of drugs;
2. the driver did not hold a valid driving licence;
3. the vehicle was used for racing, speed trials or a track day;
4. the damage was caused intentionally by the policyholder;
5. the damage results only from wear or mechanical failure.

An exclusion is never applied automatically. When a claim shows a possible
exclusion, it is referred to a claims handler, who decides.

---

### Article 4 — Reporting a claim

1. A claim must be reported within **5 days** of the incident.
   A theft must be reported within **2 days** of its discovery.
2. Every claim must state the date, the place and a description of the
   incident.
3. The following documents are required:

| Coverage | Required documents |
|----------|--------------------|
| C2 Collision | Photos of the damage; repair quote; accident report signed by both drivers when a third party is involved |
| C3 Glass | Photos of the damage; repair quote |
| C4 Theft | Police report |

A claim with a missing document is not refused: the missing document is
requested from the policyholder.

---

### Article 5 — Deductible rules

1. One deductible applies per claim.
2. **Not-at-fault waiver**: for C2, no deductible at all applies (including
   the surcharge in 5.3) when the authorised driver is not at fault **and**
   the third party is identified.
3. **Young-driver surcharge**: for C2, an additional **€400** deductible
   applies when the driver has held a driving licence for **less than 3
   years** on the day of the incident.

---

### Article 6 — Assessment and payment

1. For damage claims (C2 and C3), an independent assessor must inspect the
   vehicle when the repair cost is **above €1,500**.
2. Compensation = repair cost, capped at the market value, minus the
   deductible. Compensation is never negative.
3. Payment is made within 15 days after the compensation is approved by a
   Veridia claims handler.

---

### Article 7 — Referral to a claims handler

The following claims are referred to a Veridia claims handler, who takes the
decision:

1. a claim reported after the deadline in Article 4.1;
2. a claim that shows a possible exclusion (Article 3);
3. a claim under a coverage that is not in the Policy Schedule (Article 2).

A late claim is never refused automatically.
